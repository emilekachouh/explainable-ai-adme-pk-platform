# Reference Sources

These sources ground the app's PK/ADME educational language. They do not validate this app for clinical, regulatory, safety, efficacy, dose, PBPK, or human PK prediction.

## Regulatory and Guidance Sources

- FDA CDER PBPK Program  
  https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/cder-offices-and-divisions/program-physiologically-based-pharmacokinetic-and-pharmacodynamic-modeling-pbpk-program  
  FDA describes PBPK models as integrating drug and physiology information in mathematical models and notes predict-learn-confirm use in development contexts.

- FDA Physiologically Based Pharmacokinetic Analyses - Format and Content guidance  
  https://www.fda.gov/media/128793/download  
  Verify current document metadata before publication.

- FDA Bioavailability Studies Submitted in NDAs or INDs - General Considerations  
  https://www.fda.gov/media/121311/download  
  FDA guidance for BA information and systemic exposure study considerations.

- EMA guideline on reporting PBPK modelling and simulation  
  https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/reporting-physiologically-based-pharmacokinetic-pbpk-modelling-simulation-scientific-guideline  
  EMA describes expected PBPK report content and qualification documentation for regulatory submissions.

- EMA clinical pharmacology and pharmacokinetics questions and answers  
  https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/human-regulatory-overview/research-development/scientific-guidelines/clinical-pharmacology-pharmacokinetics-guidelines/clinical-pharmacology-pharmacokinetics-questions-answers  
  Includes PK discussion of AUC, Cmax, CL/F, NCA, and extrapolated AUC principles.

## Boundary

This app currently demonstrates permeability-informed educational absorption scenarios. It does not perform validated IVIVE, PBPK, human PK prediction, clinical dose selection, safety prediction, efficacy prediction, or regulatory decision support.
