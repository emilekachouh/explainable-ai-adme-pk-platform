# LinkedIn Post Caption

I built an open-source AI-assisted ADME screening platform combining cheminformatics, machine learning, explainability, and educational pharmacokinetics.

Key features:

- Real public Caco-2 permeability dataset
- RDKit descriptors and Morgan fingerprints
- Random split and Bemis-Murcko scaffold split validation
- SHAP explainability
- Confidence scoring and applicability-domain warnings
- Streamlit molecular screening app
- Educational PK/NCA simulator for AUC, AUMC, MRT, clearance, half-life, and extrapolated AUC

Important scientific boundary: this is not clinical PK prediction. It is an explainable early discovery screening and computational pharmacology education prototype.

This project reflects my interest in AI for drug discovery, pharmacology, and translational modeling.
